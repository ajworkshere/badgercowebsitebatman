Delete your BadgerFit account

How to delete your BadgerFit account and associated beta data, or request help by email.

Who operates BadgerFit

BadgerFit is developed and operated by The Badger Co., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. You can request account deletion through the app or by email.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

1. Open BadgerFit and sign in.

2. Open Profile → Manage Data → Delete account.

3. Review the permanent-deletion warning and confirm that you want to continue.

4. Type DELETE when prompted, then confirm. Deletion cannot be undone.

Wait for the app to report the outcome. After successful deletion, the app clears account-related data from the current browser and signs you out. If deletion fails, follow the error message or contact support.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app or sign in, email support@thebadgerco.in from the email address registered with your BadgerFit account. Use the subject BadgerFit Account Deletion Request and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.

Send an account deletion request. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Do not include your password or sign-in codes.

What account deletion covers

The deletion process removes your sign-in account and associated beta records from active account storage: profile details, 18+ eligibility confirmation, saved shoes, Fit Check cases, Foot Check responses, beta feedback, and historical scan records where present.

Camera Scan is not available in the invite-only beta. Historical scan records, if any, come from earlier testing.

Device data and separate requests

Account deletion is different from clearing browser data. Clearing device data alone does not delete your account or cloud-saved records. Device-only data in another browser or on another device may need to be cleared there separately.

Website update-signup emails and support correspondence are separate from app account records. Ask support@thebadgerco.in if you also want those removed.

Retention and assistance

Deletion of active account records does not promise immediate erasure of every infrastructure log, backup, or record needed for security or legal obligations. For retention questions or help with a deletion request, contact support@thebadgerco.in.
