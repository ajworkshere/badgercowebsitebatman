About The Badger Co.

We started with an idea to sell shoes. It grew into a wider purpose: helping people understand how products fit them, recognise when a product may be the problem, and make better decisions.

Fit goes beyond shoe size.

Width, shape, pressure and how you use a pair of shoes all affect how it fits. Many people learn to put up with discomfort or blame their feet when a shoe fits poorly.

We want to create formal footwear that looks elegant and feels comfortable. As part of that work, we want to study how feet vary across people in India and how footwear can better serve their needs.

Our first product

BadgerFit

BadgerFit is an educational web app for men. It helps you explore shoe discomfort, track your experiences, recognise patterns and make more informed footwear decisions.

Where we want to go

Over time, we want to use research, user feedback and data to develop better solutions. We’re interested in other products people use in their professional lives, and footwear is where we’re beginning.
