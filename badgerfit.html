Keep track of what changes.

My Shoes / Collection

Save the pairs you wear, revisit their Fit Checks, and log what you tried and how the shoe felt afterward. Compare two saved pairs using their own Fit Check histories.

Insights

Review your saved checks and see what you’ve reported more than once. Shoe-fit patterns come from Fit Checks. Everyday foot patterns come from Foot Checks.

Shopper’s Deck

Bring what you’ve learned to your next try-on. When there’s saved check information to draw from, Shopper’s Deck offers a practical checklist of what to look for, what to test, and what to avoid.
